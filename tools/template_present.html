Introduction

Arief & Herlina

This page presents the story of Arief and Herlina, who met at Universitas Padjadjaran (Unpad) and subsequently resolved to commit to one another.

It serves as a record of our individual backgrounds and achievements, the experiences we have shared, and the commitments we have made.

A commitment letter from Arief to Herlina

DearHerlina Mawarni,

I chose the rose because it resembles how I have come to know you. It opens gradually and without hurry, and the more of it I see, the more there is to appreciate.

It stands for what I value in you: your composure, your kindness, and a constancy I have come to rely on. It also stands for my own, which is meant for you alone.

I write this as a statement of intent. I take our promise seriously, and I intend to keep it carefully and in full until the day we can state it formally, as a vow before God.

Until then, I ask for your patience. I am working toward that day.

With sincerity and commitment,Muhammad Arief Mulyana
